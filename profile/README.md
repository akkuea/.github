## What is Akkuea

Akkuea makes rental income from real estate investable in smaller, transparent pieces. An allied real estate agency issues a tokenized right to a share of a specific property's rental income. Investors buy in at whatever size they choose, hold their position in their own Stellar wallet, and receive a pro rata share of each monthly distribution, with an on chain record of what was collected and paid out.

The current pilot is intentionally narrow in scope: a non transferable revenue participation token, not fractional equity or title. Income evidence is human reviewed and referenced on chain as a link plus a cryptographic hash, so the product is honestly described as verifiable and auditable rather than fully trustless. Alongside the pilot, the repository also hosts a larger platform build: a fractional property tokenization contract, a collateralized DeFi lending protocol, a KYC compliance engine, and Akkuea Land, an on chain game that lets anyone feel the buy property, earn income, claim income loop before real capital is involved.

## What we are building

- **Tokenized rental income** through a payout split contract on Stellar, with transparent fees and on chain distribution records
- **A collateralized DeFi lending protocol**, with oracle backed asset valuation and automated liquidation
- **Wallet based authentication and compliance**, including role gated access and an on chain audit trail
- **Akkuea Land**, a playable, fully on chain companion that mirrors the pilot's real economics

Built on **Stellar** and **Soroban**, with a modern application stack spanning Next.js, TypeScript, and Rust.
